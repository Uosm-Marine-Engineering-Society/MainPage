The project

One boat. A complete engineering programme.

ARUS I brings together marine, mechanical, electrical, control, manufacturing, safety, finance, and communications work. The team manages the same constraints faced by professional engineering programmes, with real deadlines, design reviews, testing gates, and stakeholder reporting.

From concept to competition readiness

The team is developing an energy-efficient competition boat within the Energy Class framework. Work covers rules interpretation, concept selection, detailed design, propulsion integration, battery and electrical systems, cockpit and safety systems, dry testing, water testing, transport, and competition preparation.

The programme gives students a platform to apply engineering judgement, document decisions, manage risk, work across disciplines, and communicate progress to sponsors and the public.

Engineering scope

Designed as one integrated system.

The boat is not a collection of separate components. Performance, safety, weight, cooling, control, reliability, and manufacturability must work together.

01

Hull and structure

Hydrodynamic concept, structural integration, cockpit arrangement, load paths, materials, and manufacturing planning.

02

Propulsion

Motor, controller, propeller, shaft or pod integration, mechanical supports, efficiency, and service access.

03

Energy and control

Battery architecture, BMS, fusing, isolation, low-voltage systems, telemetry, monitoring, and controls.

04

Safety and testing

Protected enclosures, emergency stop, kill cord, charging controls, inspections, dry tests, and controlled water tests.

Energy Class design constraint The programme is being developed within competition rules that include a maximum stored-energy limit of 10 kWh. Final technical values remain subject to design review and validation.
Roadmap

A phased route from rules to race day.

Each stage produces a decision, drawing, calculation, prototype, test result, or review package. The next stage starts only when the team has enough evidence to move forward.
